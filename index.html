About Us

10 Years Ago…

Raizon began as a single sewing machine in a Abuja bedroom and a promise to make clothes that actually fit. A decade on, that promise has grown into an atelier, an app, and an academy — but the hands behind every stitch are still the ones that started it all.

Today we dress brides, build wardrobes, and train the next generation of pattern cutters — all under one name, and all under one roof.

Raizon Measure

A Fit, Taken From Anywhere

Our app guides you through eleven self-measurements at home, checks each one against your body's proportions in real time, and sends your sizing straight to the pattern cutter's table. No tape-measure guesswork, no travelling in for a fitting before a single stitch is cut.

Raizon Academy

Learn the Craft, Properly

A hands-on training ground for pattern drafting, draping, beadwork and finishing, taught by the same hands behind every Raizon gown. Cohorts are small, the workroom is real, and every graduate leaves with a portfolio piece and a seat at the table.
